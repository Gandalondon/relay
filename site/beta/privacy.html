Privacy policy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your quest progress stays on your phone. Your voice is turned into text by your phone’s speech service.

Microphone

Relay asks for microphone permission the first time you tap to talk. It listens after you tap, or after you say “Hey Relay”. After each answer it listens once more, for about 8 seconds, in case you have a follow-up. It never listens while it’s speaking.

“Hey Relay” works only while Relay is open on your screen, and you can turn it off in Settings. A small model built into the app listens for just those two words, on your phone. Nothing it hears is saved, transcribed or sent anywhere. You can turn the microphone permission off at any time in your phone’s settings.

Speech recognition and voice

Relay uses Android’s built-in speech recognition to turn what you say into text. On most phones, including Pixel, that service is provided by Google. Relay asks for it to run on your phone, using Android’s offline English, which Relay can prompt you to download. If your phone doesn’t have that, audio may be processed on Google’s servers under Google’s privacy terms. Relay only receives the text.

Spoken answers use Relay’s own voice, Supertonic, which runs entirely on your phone. It is downloaded once (about 130 MB) and after that needs no internet, so nothing Relay says is sent anywhere. If you haven’t downloaded it, Relay uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech voice instead (on Pixel, Google’s).

How answers are made

Answers are worked out on your phone from game data built into Relay. On phones that have it, Relay may also use Gemini Nano, the AI built into Android, to understand what you meant; it runs on your phone too. Your questions are not sent to us or to any online AI service.

What Relay keeps

On your phone only: the quest and step you’re on, the last answer, where you last said or pinned you were, and any time left you mentioned, plus your voice and speaking speed settings. That’s how Relay picks up where you left off. There is no conversation history beyond the last answer. Tap the close icon to clear it all.

What Relay connects to

When a map is shown, Relay loads its images from MetaForge, so MetaForge receives a normal web request from your phone. The voice is downloaded once from the sherpa-onnx project on GitHub, which receives a normal download request. Nothing else: no analytics, and no account needed.

On this site, the Share Relay page plays the trailers through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Loading that page sends YouTube a normal web request; YouTube only sets cookies if you press play.

Feedback

If you email us feedback, we receive your message and your email address, so we can reply. We use them for nothing else.
