How it works

Put your phone beside you and talk to it. Relay answers out loud, so your eyes stay on the game.

Using Relay

Ask out loud

Say “Hey Relay” or tap the yellow button, then ask. Relay answers out loud and listens once more for a follow-up.

Tell it where you are

Say which map you’re on, like “Show me the Blue Gate map”, then a place, like “I’m at the Village”. Or say both at once. Can’t remember the name? Tap the locate button, tap your spot on the map, then tell it what you’re looking for.

Find the nearest anything

Ask for an enemy, a raider cache, a plant like moss or apricots, or something to craft with, like wires. Relay names the nearest, says which way and how far, and puts one yellow dot on the map with a dashed line from you. No filters to set.

Get out

Ask for the nearest exit and it names the way out, which way to head and how far. Not that one? Say so and it finds the next. Short on time? Say how many minutes are left and it keeps it brief. Have a hatch key? Mention you have one and it may find you a nearer exit.

Quest steps (in testing)

Tell it the quest and step, like “I’m on Home to Roost, step 3”. Say “Done” and it moves you on and shows the next spot. Quest steps are still in testing, so tell us when one is wrong.

See the whole map

Tap the expand button on the map to see all of it, and again to go back to the answer. Red outlines are Abundant loot and yellow ones Decent, as in the game.

Voices

Download the voices

Open Settings from the cog on the home screen. The natural voices are a one-off 130 MB download. Until then, Relay uses your phone’s voice.

Pick one

Choose from female and male voices. Tap the speaker to hear each one, and set the speaking speed to Normal, Fast or Faster.

Things to say

Where you are

  • “I’m at the Village”
  • “I’m on the east side”
  • “Show me the Dam map”
  • “Show me Blue Gate, I’m at the Village”

Finding things

  • “Nearest Bastion”
  • “Where’s the nearest raider cache?”
  • “Any apricots near me?”
  • “Where do I find wires?”
  • “I need rubber parts”
  • “I’m on Blue Gate, south-east, find me a Sentinel”

Getting out

  • “Where’s the nearest exit?”
  • “Not that one”
  • “I can’t go north-east”
  • “Two minutes left, get me out”
  • “Any hatches near me?”

Quests

  • “I’m on Home to Roost, step 3”
  • “Done”
  • “Where do I go?”

A few things to know

Relay can’t see your game. It only knows where you are when you tell it, so tell it again when you start a new raid.

Relay works best with clear, specific questions. Its answers come from community game data, so they aren’t always perfect. If something doesn’t sound right, try asking another way.

Maps and locations come from MetaForge and community guides, and some positions are approximate. All seven maps are in: Blue Gate, Dam Battlegrounds, Buried City, Spaceport, Stella Montis, Riven Tides and Pendola Pass.

Answers and voices run on your phone, so once the voices are downloaded Relay can talk without a connection. Maps need one to load.
